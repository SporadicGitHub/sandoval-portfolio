How I approach a brief

Good design starts with the message.

The campaign work above began with an offer that needed to be communicated clearly. I handled the visual composition and hierarchy using photography supplied by the client.

01

Clarify

Identify the offer, audience-facing information, and the action the graphic needs to communicate.

02

Organize

Build a hierarchy for the key message, supporting details, imagery, and call to action.

03

Deliver

Prepare a consistent set of promotional layouts and review the result as a campaign, not isolated posts.

This describes the design approach applied to the project; no campaign-performance figures are claimed.
